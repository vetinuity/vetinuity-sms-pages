1. Program Description

Vetinuity Scheduling Notifications is an SMS messaging service operated by Vetinuity. The service sends operational, non-marketing text messages to employees of veterinary clinics that use the Vetinuity scheduling platform. Messages are sent on behalf of your employer to keep you informed about your work schedule.

2. Consent and Opt-In

By providing your mobile phone number to your employer for use with the Vetinuity platform, and by confirming your opt-in when you receive your first message, you consent to receive scheduling-related SMS messages from Vetinuity.

Consent is not a condition of employment. You may decline to receive SMS notifications and instead view your schedule through the web application.

3. Message Types

You may receive the following types of messages:

  • Callout Alerts - Notifications when a shift needs immediate coverage.
  • Shift Claim Confirmations - Confirmation when a shift has been claimed.
  • Schedule Publications - Notifications when a new schedule is published.
  • Schedule Changes - Alerts when your assigned shifts have been modified.
  • PTO Updates - Status changes on your time-off requests.
  • Escalation Notices - Alerts when shift coverage is being escalated to additional staff.

4. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on scheduling activity at your workplace. During active scheduling periods (e.g., new schedule publication, callout events), you may receive multiple messages per day. During quiet periods, you may receive no messages for days at a time. This is not a recurring daily messaging program.

5. Costs

Vetinuity does not charge you for receiving SMS messages. However, standard message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply. Contact your carrier for details about your messaging plan.

6. Opt-Out

You may opt out of SMS notifications at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any message from Vetinuity.
  • Asking your manager to disable SMS notifications for your account.

After opting out, you will receive a single confirmation message. No further messages will be sent unless you opt back in.

7. Opt Back In

To resume receiving SMS notifications, you may:

  • Reply START or SUBSCRIBE to our number.
  • Ask your manager to re-enable SMS notifications in the platform.

8. Help

For help with SMS notifications, you may:

  • Reply HELP to any message for support information.
  • Contact your manager or clinic administrator.
  • Email us at support@vetinuity.com.

9. Privacy

Your phone number and messaging data are handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or opt-in status with third parties for marketing purposes.
