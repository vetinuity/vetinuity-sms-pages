Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 14, 2026

Vetinuity ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Vetinuity Scheduling platform, a workforce scheduling application for veterinary clinics. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information, including information related to our SMS messaging services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, employee ID, and role as provided by your employer.
  • Phone Number: Your mobile phone number, provided by your employer or by you, used to send scheduling-related SMS notifications.
  • Scheduling Data: Shift assignments, availability preferences, PTO requests, skills, and work history within the platform.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the platform, including login times and feature usage.

2. SMS Messaging

Vetinuity uses SMS text messaging to send operational scheduling notifications to employees of our client organizations. By opting in to SMS notifications, you consent to receive the following types of messages:

  • Callout Alerts: Notifications when a shift needs coverage.
  • Shift Claim Confirmations: Confirmation when you or a coworker claims a shift.
  • Schedule Updates: Notifications when your schedule is published or changed.
  • PTO Updates: Status updates on your time-off requests.
  • Escalation Notices: Alerts when shift coverage is being escalated to additional staff.

Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on scheduling activity. You may receive multiple messages per week during active scheduling periods.

Message and Data Rates: Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-Out: You can opt out of SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive a confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent. You may also contact your manager to remove your phone number from the system.

Opt-In: You can re-subscribe to SMS notifications by replying START to our number or by requesting your manager re-enable SMS notifications in the platform.

Help: Reply HELP for support information.

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or SMS opt-in/opt-out status with third parties for marketing purposes. Phone numbers are used solely for delivering scheduling-related notifications through our platform.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the scheduling platform on behalf of your employer.
  • Send SMS notifications related to your work schedule.
  • Process shift assignments, callouts, and PTO requests.
  • Improve the platform's functionality and user experience.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • With Your Employer: Your scheduling data is accessible to authorized managers at your organization.
  • Service Providers: We use third-party services (such as Twilio for SMS delivery) that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular security assessments.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your employer maintains an active account with us, or as required by law. SMS messaging logs are retained for up to 12 months for compliance purposes.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting your employer's administrator or by contacting us directly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify affected users of material changes via the platform or email.
